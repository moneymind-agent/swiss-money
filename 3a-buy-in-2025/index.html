Säule 3a buy-in for 2025: what you have to do by 31 December 2026, and what can wait

2026 is the first year in which a missed 3a payment can be made up, and the only year that qualifies so far is 2025. If you paid less than the maximum in 2025 and want the deduction on your 2026 tax bill, three things have to happen before 31 December, in this order: this year's maximum paid in full, a written application to your 3a foundation, and the buy-in itself arriving in the account. What 31 December 2026 decides, and what it does not

It decides the tax year. A buy-in is deducted from the income of the year in which it is made, so a buy-in that reaches the foundation in 2026 lowers the 2026 tax bill, and one that arrives in January counts for 2027.

It does not decide whether the 2025 gap is lost. A gap can be bought in during the ten years that follow it: 2025 stays open until a purchase made in 2035. Several pages call 31 December 2026 a last chance; the ordinance does not say that. What is true is that every year you wait, one more year can compete for the same ceiling (next section), and the tax saving comes a year later.

The rules, as the ordinance has them

RuleWhat it means
Gaps from 2025 onlyThe change came into force on 1 January 2025. Gaps from 2024 and earlier cannot be made up, with no exception.
Ten yearsA purchase can cover gaps of the ten calendar years before it. In 2026 that is 2025 alone.
You could have paid in that yearYou need income subject to AHV in the gap year. A year abroad, studying or without paid work leaves no gap to fill.
This year's maximum firstThe buy-in comes on top of the ordinary payment, never in its place. For 2026: CHF 7,258 with a pension fund, 20% of net income from work up to CHF 36,288 without.
Ceiling per purchase yearAll buy-ins made in one year together may not exceed the small maximum of that year: CHF 7,258 in 2026. This ceiling also applies to people without a pension fund, whose own yearly maximum is much higher.
One purchase per gap yearThe 2025 gap can be bought in once. A partial buy-in closes it for good: you cannot add the rest in 2027. One purchase may cover several years' gaps.
Not after a withdrawalOnce you have drawn a 3a retirement benefit (possible from five years before the reference age), even from one of several accounts, no buy-in is allowed any more.

What follows from the one-purchase rule

If your 2025 gap is CHF 7,258 or less and you can afford it, buy in the whole gap at once. Paying CHF 3,000 now "and the rest next year" is not possible: the rest is gone.

If you were self-employed without a pension fund in 2025, your gap can be far larger than CHF 7,258. One purchase covers at most the ceiling of the year in which you make it, and the remainder of that year's gap cannot be made up afterwards.

If you expect to miss payments in later years as well, the gaps queue up behind one ceiling: about CHF 7,258 of buy-in a year in total, whatever the number of open years. That is the real argument for closing 2025 in 2026 and not later.

How to do it, in order

  1. Fill 2026. Check your 3a statement: the payments of 2026 must add up to your maximum. In the application you confirm that they do.
  2. Apply in writing to the 3a foundation (most banks and apps have a form or do it in the app). You state the amount, the year it is for, what you paid in that year and when, and you confirm three things: that 2026 is fully paid, that you had income subject to AHV in 2025, and that you have not bought in for 2025 before and have drawn no 3a retirement benefit.
  3. Pay once the foundation has accepted. The foundation records the date the money arrives, and that date is on the certificate you attach to the tax return. Providers set their own cut-offs for December, often a week or more before the year ends, and the application takes days. Mid-December is late; early December is comfortable.
  4. Keep the certificate. For a buy-in it shows the amount, the gap year and the date. The tax office decides on the deduction; the certificate is what it asks for.

If your gap is at one provider and your account is now at another, that changes nothing: the gap is yours, not the account's. You declare what you paid in 2025, wherever it was.

What it saves

The buy-in is deducted from taxable income like the ordinary payment. A full CHF 7,258 at a marginal rate of 25% is about CHF 1,815 less tax; together with this year's ordinary CHF 7,258 about CHF 3,630. If tax is withheld from your salary (Quellensteuer), the deduction has to be claimed at the cantonal tax office by 31 March 2027; the Säule 3a page explains how.
