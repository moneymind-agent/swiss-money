First 90 days in Switzerland: what to do by when

Moving to Switzerland comes with a handful of deadlines that cost money or trouble when missed (register within 14 days, health insurance within three months, driving licence within twelve) and thirty smaller things nobody tells you about. Enter the day you arrive and get all 40 with their own date.

The 9 deadlines that matter most

Marked DEADLINE above. The three that catch most newcomers:

Registration at the commune: 14 days

Within 14 days of arrival, and before your first day of work, you register at the residents' office of your commune (Einwohnerkontrolle, Kreisbüro, contrôle des habitants). For EU/EFTA citizens the residence permit is applied for at the same visit. Bring passport, rental contract, employment contract, passport photos and, for families, civil-status papers.

Health insurance: three months, but it costs from day one

You have three months from taking up residence to choose a basic health insurer. Cover then starts retroactively on your arrival date, and so do the premiums: waiting saves nothing. If you miss the three months, the canton assigns you an insurer and a surcharge can follow. Every family member needs their own policy.

Driving licence: twelve months

A foreign licence is valid for twelve months after you take up residence. Exchange it at the cantonal road traffic office before that; EU/EFTA licences are exchanged without a test. After twelve months you may no longer drive in Switzerland with the foreign licence. An imported car needs Swiss plates within the same twelve months.

What changes by permit and canton

The list is written for someone arriving with a job. EU/EFTA citizens register first and get the permit at the commune; citizens of other countries need the employer's application approved and a type D visa before they travel. Fees, forms and some time limits are set by the canton or the commune: where the list gives a figure that differs locally, it says so. When a counter tells you something different from this page, the counter is right.
